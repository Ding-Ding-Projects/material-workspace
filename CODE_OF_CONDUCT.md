# Code of conduct

Be decent to people. Criticise code, never contributors.

Harassment, personal attacks, and discrimination of any kind are not welcome
here — in issues, pull requests, discussions, or anywhere else this project is
discussed.

Humour is welcome, and it is aimed at the work: a build that broke in a stupid
way, a defect that survived three reviews, a design decision that aged badly. It
is never aimed at a person, an ethnicity, a nationality, an accent, an age, a
class of person, or how somebody speaks or writes.

If something here is making the project worse to be part of, open an issue or
contact the maintainers privately. Reports are taken seriously and handled as
discreetly as the situation allows.
